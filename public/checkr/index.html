Pre-hire is mature, and Checkr already serves the largest gig platforms. The growth is after the hire, and four in five employers have not started.

A $55 check is paid once. Base monitoring is $20 a year per worker; a compliance tier is $40+, for as long as they stay.

3 / 5 · The bar · from who we serve to what we build

Compliance-grade means a surveyor accepts it as proof

A Joint Commission surveyor, a federal auditor, or a plaintiff's lawyer. Three things have to be true.

Evidence

Every event recorded: consent, source, time, rule, notice, dispute, outcome.

Reporting out of the box

The monthly packet to the accreditor, and the logs a surveyor asks for, are exports of the record.

Enforced process

Same alert, same handling. The FCRA process is automated and cannot be skipped.

4 / 5 · Distribution

The process lives in Checkr. Partners get the outcome.

The process stays enforced

Enforced process is the third part of compliance-grade. It cannot depend on each partner building it.

A small ask of partners

One status to consume, not rules, notices and disputes to build. Less to stall on their roadmap.

Checkr stays the source of truth

The full record lives with us. If a firm changes its ATS, the history is intact.

Assumption to validate: how much of this work firms do in our dashboard versus in their ATS.

5 / 5 · Prioritization

Now, next, later: each step unlocks the next segment

Now
Unlocks: accredited staffing firms, paying for the compliance tier
  • Evidence record: audit view (the screen a surveyor sees) and exports
  • Decision layer: Assess extended to license and exclusion events; rule templates with the FCRA steps locked
  • In the Checkr dashboard
Next
Unlocks: the rest of healthcare staffing, home health and home care
  • State registries: aides and nursing assistants (ours to build)
  • Clearance status pushed to Bullhorn, WellSky, as BD lands them
  • Compliance tier on the price list and marketplaces
Later
Unlocks: fleets and delivery, where Checkr MVR already sells
  • FMCSA Clearinghouse queries, run on the fleet's behalf
  • Submit the monthly packet for them, not just export it
  • Clearance status in AxisCare, AlayaCare

Arrest feed

No healthcare mandate requires it. Every vendor can buy the same feed. Many states restrict employment use of non-conviction records.

Childcare

Five-year re-check cycle, run through state portals, and a shrinking workforce.

Legal won't allow mandatory FCRA steps

Make them the default with logged overrides.

Design partners place mostly aides

State registries move into Now.

Firms work in Bullhorn, not the dashboard

Status push to Bullhorn moves into Now; it is one field and one webhook.
